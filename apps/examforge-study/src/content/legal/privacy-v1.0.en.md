Depending on how you use the Service, we may process:

- **Account data:** email address, display name, account identifier, verification status, and age declaration;

- **Support data:** messages, files, and contact information that you choose to submit;

- **Browser storage:** essential cookies, language/theme choices, cached profile data, and attempt drafts where that feature is enabled.

ExamForge does not request payment data in the current MVP. If payments are introduced, this Policy and the provider register will be updated before related data is collected.
