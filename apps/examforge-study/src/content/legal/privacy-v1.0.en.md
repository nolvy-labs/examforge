# ExamForge Privacy Policy

- **Version:** 1.0
- **Effective date:** August 10, 2026
- **Last updated:** August 10, 2026

## 1. Who operates ExamForge

ExamForge is operated by **Trần Ngọc Lâm Vỹ**.

ExamForge determines why and how personal data described in this Policy is processed and is responsible for that processing to the extent required by applicable Vietnamese law.

Privacy requests may be sent to **vy.tranngoclam@gmail.com**.

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies when you:

* access ExamForge;
* use an ExamForge account;
* take or review an exam or practice attempt;
* view learning statistics;
* communicate with ExamForge; or
* otherwise use the Service.

Independent third-party websites or services linked from ExamForge are governed by their own privacy practices.

## 3. Personal data we process

Depending on your use of the Service, ExamForge may process the following categories.

### Account data

* email address;
* display name;
* internal user identifier;
* account role and account status.

ExamForge does not currently require a date of birth or age declaration as part of its standard account data.

### Authentication data

* password hash;
* access and refresh session information;
* token expiry and revocation information;
* authentication-related timestamps and events.

### Learning and examination data

* exams and versions accessed;
* attempt identifiers and mode;
* answers and selected options;
* progress and saved responses;
* attempt timing information where applicable;
* grading information;
* scores;
* attempt status and history;
* statistics derived from attempts.

### Technical and security data

Systems involved in providing ExamForge may process information such as:

* IP address;
* HTTP request information;
* browser or user-agent information;
* timestamps;
* application and server logs;
* error information; and
* security-related events.

### Browser-side data

ExamForge currently uses browser-side storage for:

* language preferences; and
* recovery of unsynchronized examination-attempt data.

Further details are provided in the Cookie and Client Storage Policy.

### Communications

If you contact ExamForge, we may process the information you provide in that communication.

## 4. Data we do not currently use

The current ExamForge service does not intentionally use personal data for:

* personalized advertising;
* sale of personal data;
* behavioral advertising;
* advertising profiles; or
* marketing tracking.

The Service does not currently require payment-card information.

If this changes, this Policy will be updated before the relevant processing is introduced where required.

## 5. Why we process personal data

Personal data may be processed to:

* create and administer accounts;
* authenticate users and maintain sessions;
* save examination attempts and answers;
* grade submitted attempts;
* display results and learning statistics;
* recover unsynchronized attempt data;
* secure accounts and the Service;
* prevent abuse;
* investigate errors and incidents;
* provide support;
* comply with applicable legal requirements; and
* maintain and improve the reliability of the Service.

Processing is carried out with consent or another basis permitted by applicable Vietnamese law, depending on the processing activity.

Where consent is legally required, withdrawing consent does not invalidate processing that lawfully occurred before withdrawal.

## 6. Service providers and disclosures

ExamForge may use infrastructure and technology providers to operate the Service, including providers of:

* frontend hosting and content delivery;
* backend/server hosting;
* databases;
* transactional email;
* networking and security; and
* backup or infrastructure services where configured.

These providers may process only the information reasonably necessary to provide their service to ExamForge.

ExamForge does not sell personal data.

Personal data may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary:

* to comply with a lawful request from a competent authority;
* to protect the security or integrity of the Service;
* to investigate fraud, abuse or unlawful activity; or
* to protect legal rights.

## 7. Processing outside Vietnam

Some technology providers may operate infrastructure outside Vietnam.

Where personal data is processed or transferred across borders, ExamForge will handle such processing in accordance with applicable Vietnamese requirements governing personal-data protection and cross-border processing.

Provider and deployment locations may change as infrastructure changes.

## 8. Retention

ExamForge keeps personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose or as required by applicable law.

In general:

* account information and learning history may be retained while an account remains active;
* attempt data may be retained to provide history, results and statistics;
* access-token sessions are currently short-lived;
* refresh sessions expire or may be revoked;
* security and operational records are retained only for operational, security, troubleshooting or legal needs;
* communications may be retained while a request or dispute is being handled; and
* infrastructure backups, where used, may retain deleted information until the relevant backup is overwritten or expires.

A deletion request does not necessarily require immediate deletion of every record where retention is required or permitted for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or backup purposes.
